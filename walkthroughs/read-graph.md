# Read the graph

Each row is one commit, newest first. The coloured lines on the left show how branches diverge and merge.

- **Labels** on a row name the branches, tags and remote branches that point at that commit. A branch label also shows how far it is ahead of or behind its upstream.
- The **checked-out branch** is bold, and its commit carries a marker in the left margin.
- **Uncommitted changes** appears as the top row, with the number of files involved, when your working tree has changes. Click it to browse the staged, unstaged, untracked and conflicted files and open their diffs or merge editor.
- Click a commit to open its **details**: message, author, dates and the changed files. Click a file to open its diff.
- **Load Older Commits** at the bottom extends the history. The status strip above the graph shows the current branch, its upstream, and any merge or rebase in progress.

Drag the column borders to resize them. The date format and other options live in the extension settings, reachable from the settings cog.
